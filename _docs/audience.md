# Audience

## Chatbot Users

Stitch is a better chatbot interface: a place to converse, share, and grow ideas organically.
* Organize your threads with tags, links, and subjects
* Add your friends to your chatbot conversations
* Experiment with multiple models in one place
* Test the difference between different prompts
* Leverage full multimodal attachment support

## Teams

* Collaborate with AI as a team in a group context
* Standardize and share AI prompts and contexts, including messages relevant to your documents, database, codebase, or future plans
* Audit and share AI workflows for team education and collaborative learning
* Integrate custom bots, including for document reference, code changes, calendar organization, and more

## AI Researchers (Academic & Industrial)

* Benchmark, compare, and study different AI models collaboratively
* Track and persist results of systematic test suites for comparing models and prompt, timestamped for reference
* Deploy your own model on Stitch for easy testing and sharing

## Developers

* Stitch offers a framework and a platform for developing grounded AI agents that operate as first-class participants in conversations
* Build agents with persistent identities that maintain relationships and context across sessions.  
* Build proactive agents that can dynamically access their own conversation history
* Monetize valuable agents through usage-based billing models that reward developers for creating economically valuable autonomous systems (eventually)
* Enable humans in the loop for tasks with long timelines: your bot just sends them a message and they get a notification for decision points and validation checks
