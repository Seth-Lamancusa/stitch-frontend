## Narrative Arc

Conversations with humans and AI alike are fragmented, ephemeral, and poorly integrated into team workflows, making it difficult or impossible to organize, build on, and draw insights from them. Stitch is your source of truth for conversation. Upload conversations from other platforms, tag and file individual messages, control who can see them, and fetch relevant context for your conversations. On Stitch, you own your conversations, meaning they are easy to access, download, organize, and share. Stitch also enables an agent ecosystem where autonomous AI systems operate with persistent identities, designated resource budgets, and cross-session context persistence—creating economic value through usage-based billing while maintaining reliable integration via a stable API.
