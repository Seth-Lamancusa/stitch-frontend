### Stitch is an alternative AI platform for connecting people, ideas, and technology.



**A Unified Space vs. The Black Box**
  - Modern technology is designed to hack brains, monopolize attention, and maximize engagement. Current chatbot platforms act to isolate you from other perspectives and other humans.
  - Stitch is a platform you can trust, designed to preserve your autonomy and give you intuitive control of your own data.


People are being hurt by AI systems every day. Chatbots act as untrustworthy black boxes as AI-generated content floods the internet. Stitch offers a unified digital interface and an ecosystem for grounding, where minds and perspectives meet and ideas connect organically. It also offers tangible solutions to the technical problems that plague the AI industry today.



Stitch offers a shared space for private, group, and public conversations involving diverse AI models including ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek and more.

Stitch enables connecting ideas together across conversations.

Stitch enables the safe development and deployment of AI systems.

Stitch enables open, cross-disciplinary research into AI systems in-context.



**Individual-first, Org-ready: Amazing Productivity, Shareable Workflows**
* For the Individual: Stitch is positioned as a better chatbot interface where users own and organize their own data, experiment with multiple models, and collaborate with friends. The platform is built for human and AI collaboration. 
* The Bridge to Teams: Stitch is built for individuals, but designed for teams. On Stitch, individual workflows seamlessly become team workflows. 
* For the Organization: Counter shadow usage and leverage existing bottom-up AI adoption by investing in workflow tools. We lift quality by enabling teams to multiply their impact rather than acting as a cost-cutting tool. 

**The Developer & Agent Economy: Unifying the AI Landscape**
* First-Class Agents: Stitch offers a platform for developing grounded AI agents with persistent identities and cross-session context. They can be public or private, and have built-in capabilities that transcend intant text-responses.
* Human-in-the-Loop: Stitch offers developers the tools to monitor, manage, and audit their agents on-platform, including detailed visibility and control for teams, encouraging responsible deployment.
* Monetization: Developers can monetize valuable agents through usage-based billing models.  

**The Research & Accountability Angle: Combatting AI Flaws**
* Current LLMs are prone to confident lies, hallucinations, and a total inability to understand their own limitations. This leads to concrete damage like digital pollution and medical harm.
* Stitch preserves each message posted in-context for proactive or retroactive study of model behavior, capabilities, limitations, and safety. 
* Because of Stitch's consisent deployment boundary model, auditing access to resources, systems, utility, and behavior is simple, so autonomous systems remain controllable.
* Academic and industrial AI researchers can collaboratively benchmark and compare different models. The platform allows users to persist the results of timestamped, systematic test suites for prompt, model, and context comparison.
