## Why orgs make sense for client acquisition

1. Clear ROI and budgets: Organizations can justify spend on workflow tools; individuals often won’t pay.
2. Decision makers: Easier to identify and reach than individual users.
3. Natural fit: Your features (audit trails, visibility controls, team collaboration) align with org needs.
4. Quality-lift narrative: The article’s framing resonates with leaders who want to enable teams, not just cut costs.

## Why you shouldn’t abandon the public/technical audience

1. Product development: Technical users provide feedback, surface edge cases, and push boundaries.
2. Evangelism: Technical users often bring tools into their organizations.
3. Network effects: Public conversations showcase capabilities and build credibility.
4. The article’s insight: “Individuals out-executing teams” suggests individual power users are valuable.

## Recommended approach: “Individual-first, org-ready”

Position Stitch as a platform that works for individuals and scales to teams:

- Lead with org value prop in sales/marketing (quality lift, workflow integration, auditability).
- Keep the public platform strong (community, technical users, public conversations).
- Bridge messaging: “Built for individuals, designed for teams” or “Start with your team, scale to your organization.”
