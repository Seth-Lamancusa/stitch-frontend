# For Organizations

## Lift quality

* The real value is AI is not in cost cutting; it's in enabling new capabilities and unlocking new markets
* Stitch enables you to do something you couldn't do before, encouraging organizational growth, not pruning
* Give your existing team the ability to multiply their impact and trust your people to leverage AI creatively

## Dissolve the technical / non-technical barrier

* Teams can integrate AI workflows without deep technical expertise
* Everyone picks up the degree of technical skill they need to solve the problems they're interested in
* AI tooling enables fast iteration and an accessible interface for technical systems like databases, code, and logs

## Systems over models

* Your ability to leverage AI is not tied to your having the smartest model, but to your ability to integrate AI into your team's workflows and leverage it creatively
* Humans in the loop are not going anywhere
* Build systems where there's discipline around what gets generated with AI and how it's deployed
* Retrieval grounds the output, validation catches the errors, and humans in the loop handle edge cases and sensitive checkpoints
* Because each bot simply runs on its own machine, auditing access to resources, actions taken, behavior, and personel utility becomes a simple and easy
